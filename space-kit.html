MeshAxiom / Reusable asset library

Reusable modules,
one image per asset.

123 generated meshes with enhanced 4K–8K materials, from collector tiles and station modules to dreadnoughts, Titan bio-ships and soldiers of both sides, plus 19 assemblies built from them. Modular pieces share connector sizes, so they snap together.

The MeshAxiom-Base model repository is a private pre-release, currently limited to authorized collaborators.

MeshAxiom-Base generates explicit geometry from one reference image. Selected assets include bounded repairs; external materials combine projected reference detail and inferred surfaces, and enhanced atlases add estimated detail. Small seams and openings may remain. Assemblies, lighting and motion are authored.
