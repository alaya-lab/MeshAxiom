Why agent-native?

Geometry that tools can inspect

Compact mesh files retain explicit vertices and triangles. Agents call geometry tools and receive compact summaries and views; savings depend on the task.

Modules that scene programs can reuse

Asset records connect geometry and materials to instance transforms, rigs and motion. One module kit supports several assemblies, with matched geometry and material views.
